# Context

Grupo Eurosa is an aluminum products company with a presence in more than 13 countries. The client had a static HTML website and needed to update products, categories, and virtual tours more independently.

# My role

I worked as an independent developer. I designed and developed the new website, integrated the frontend with a WordPress CMS through GraphQL, and adapted the solution to the existing DonWeb server.

# Solution

The platform uses Next.js and is deployed on Vercel. WordPress works as the CMS, allowing the client to manage content without depending on a developer. I also developed a central endpoint and a visualization system for more than 100 devices, including a preview workflow before production deployment.

# Results

The client can independently manage products, categories, and virtual tours. SEO, overall stability, and website visits improved. I do not have verifiable sales or inquiry data, so I do not make that claim. I currently maintain the server and develop new updates according to the client's requirements.
