# Context

Over approximately two months, I developed an internal project to support sales processes originating from advertising campaigns for different companies.

# My role

I worked as an independent developer across the entire project, including the backend, AI agents, catalog, scheduling, metrics, observability, and architectural decisions.

# Solution

The MVP uses an agent system to detect intent, query an authorized catalog, assist sales opportunities, manage appointments, and record operational metrics. The goal is to provide a more personalized experience and prioritize leads with a higher likelihood of conversion, with a future vision of adding an AI-powered CRM.

# Status and confidentiality

The agent system, catalog, scheduling, Ads ingestion, metrics, and observability are operational in closed client testing. WhatsApp, multi-company support, and payment links are not operational yet. For privacy and confidentiality reasons, the code, interface screenshots, and internal implementation details are not published.
