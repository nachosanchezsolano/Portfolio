# About me

I am a Full Stack Developer specialized in AI Engineering, with a technical background in Data Science and data-driven solutions. I am also a secondary-school teacher focused on Project-Based Learning.

Since approximately 2024, I have worked as an independent developer for two active clients. I handle discovery, meetings, frontend, backend, deployment, infrastructure maintenance, SEO and analytics. I care about building useful, simple, secure, observable, and evolvable solutions.

# Experience

My main client projects include Grupo Eurosa and a dedicated exhibitor web platform, together with its infrastructure maintenance and central endpoint. From March 2021 to July 2023, I worked in a paid role at the La Falda Innovation and Technology Center, maintaining websites and developing technology-related projects. I contributed to more than ten projects and to funding proposals for institutional investment.

# Teaching

I have been teaching since August 2023. I currently teach Data Structures, Programming, Testing, Science Club, and Ethics and Legislation, working with approximately 30 students per class. One project I supported received the provincial best-project recognition at Emprende U; students worked with user stories and Scrum methodologies.

# Education

I hold a Higher Technical Degree in Data Science, completed in 2024 at Instituto Superior Politécnico de Córdoba. I also trained as a frontend developer at IES in 2021, as a Mechanical Technician, and completed Scrum Fundamentals in November 2022. I started but did not complete a Mechanical Engineering degree or a Mechatronics Technician program.
